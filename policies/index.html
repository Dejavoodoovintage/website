How buying works

  1. Press Reserve this piece and send us the request. You’ll see the price, shipping and total before you send it.
  2. Every piece is one of a kind, so we confirm it's still available before sending your invoice; the piece shows as On hold while it waits for you.
  3. Within a day we email you a PayPal invoice. Pay by PayPal, Venmo or any credit or debit card. You don’t need a PayPal account.
  4. Once paid, your piece is tissue-wrapped, boxed, insured and shipped from Santa Cruz with tracking.

Shipping

  • We ship within the United States only. Our insurance does not cover shipments overseas.
  • Everything goes by USPS with tracking, and every package is fully insured.
  • Orders over $200 ship with signature confirmation, and orders over $500 go by USPS Priority Mail.
  • One charge covers shipping and insurance. You’ll see the exact amount when you reserve a piece.

Returns

If you aren’t happy with a piece, return it within two weeks of receiving it for a full refund, minus shipping. Just email us to let us know it’s coming back.

Honest descriptions

Every piece is weighed and measured, its markings are noted, and any flaws are photographed and described. Many older Native American pieces were never stamped for silver content; we say so when that’s the case.

Want a closer look before you buy? Ask us for more photos.

Your information

Your name, email and address are used only to invoice and ship your order. We never sell or share them.
